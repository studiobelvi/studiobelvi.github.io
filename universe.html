Story Universe

This is our most comprehensive collaboration option.
If you don’t know where to start or simply have so much on your mind that you’d rather we take care of everything, with Story Universe you don’t have to worry about a thing. We build the entire narrative surrounding your wedding from scratch. After all, that’s what we’re here to do. We’re deeply interested in your story and that of your partner, and we want to support you with our expertise.

How we do it

The first step is for you to reach out to us via the contact page, briefly telling us what kind of wedding you’re planning and why you’d like us to craft the entire narrative.
From there, we’ll schedule a meeting—either online or in person—to get to know you a little better. The notes we take during the meeting will help us select the questions for a standardized form. We’ll send you the form, tailored to what we already know about you, and we’ll also include a quote for the Creative Direction Proposal.
Once you approve the aspects of the proposal that interest you, we’ll conduct a cost analysis, agree on deadlines, and send you a quote for the entire collaboration. All we’ll need is your approval and a deposit to get started building—from the ground up—the story your wedding deserves.
