Story Chapters

With this collaboration option, we take it one step further.
You may have a very clear vision for your wedding theme and have already defined your aesthetic preferences, but you want to add a narrative layer that fits the image you’ve envisioned. You may have already chosen the menu, the flowers, the ceremony decor, the centerpieces…
If that’s the case, we’ll take care of weaving your personal and couple’s story into every stage of the wedding—whether through the services you suggest or ideas we come up with—so that your essence shines through in every corner on this special day.

How we do it

If you’re sure that Story Chapters are right for you, write to us and tell us everything you’ve already planned and everything you have in mind. We’ll schedule a meeting to address any remaining questions, and if you’d like to move forward with the collaboration, we’ll send you a quote and a delivery date for the Creative Direction Proposal.
Once you’ve received that document and approved the proposals—whether in full or in part—we’ll conduct a cost analysis, agree on deadlines, and send you a quote for the entire collaboration. If it aligns with what you had in mind, our team will get to work designing and crafting all the elements that will add a touch of originality and beauty to your wedding.
But if you’d like to take it a step further and leave the entire wedding narrative in our hands—from start to finish—be sure to check out the Story Universe section.
