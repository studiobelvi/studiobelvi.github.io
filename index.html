WHAT WE DO

Your personality as a main character

We always place the utmost importance on character, originality, beauty, and emotion.
To bring your story to life, we cover three essential areas of storytelling…

01

memorabilia

These are all the elements that serve as mementos of a beautiful story. We offer your guests—as well as you and your partner—all kinds of thoughtful touches and symbolic items that reflect your personality and your life journey. Would you like to see some examples?

02

scenography

It involves the design and creation of everything that unifies the spaces under a single theme and a consistent aesthetic. The narrative atmosphere is what reflects your true essence. How do you envision the ceremony? What do the table decorations, the ceremonial arch, the altar, or the photo booth say about you?

03

immersion

This final area turns your guests into irreplaceable characters in your story. Through interactive elements that engage all five senses (games, exhibits, raffles, musical bingo, treasure hunts…), you’ll all be immersed in the narrative in which you play the leading role.
